## Common pests
Mealy bug (tukra): leaf tips curl into bunchy shape; remove and destroy affected tips, avoid moving cuttings from infested gardens. Thrips and mites cause leaf curling, silvery patches and yellowing; spray a recommended miticide and observe the waiting period before feeding leaves. Leaf roller and Bihar hairy caterpillar can defoliate young plantation; hand-pick caterpillars and destroy egg masses.

## Pesticide safety for silkworm rearing
Silkworms are extremely sensitive to pesticide residue. Leaves sprayed with chemicals must not be fed to silkworms until the waiting period printed on the product label has passed. Keep separate leaf plots for rearing, and never store pesticides in the rearing house.

## Leaf yield and stock
Mulberry leaves wilt within a day or two. Harvested leaves for rearing should be fed fresh or stored in a cool, moist, clean place for a short time only. A farmer should plan harvest to match the rearing schedule of linked silkworm farmers.
