## Powdery mildew
Cause: fungus (Phyllactinia corylea). Symptoms: white powdery patches mostly on the underside of mature leaves, leaves turn yellow and dry in cool humid weather. Mitigation: remove badly affected leaves, avoid overcrowding and improve air circulation, apply a recommended fungicide or sulphur-based spray and strictly observe the waiting period on the label before feeding leaves to silkworms. Escalation: if more than a fifth of the plot is affected, leaf quantity for the next rearing will fall; inform the reeler.

## Leaf spot
Cause: fungus (Cercospora moricola). Symptoms: small brown to dark spots with yellow halo that merge and cause premature leaf fall in the rainy season. Mitigation: collect and destroy fallen leaves, ensure drainage, balanced fertiliser (avoid excess nitrogen), spray recommended fungicide and respect the withholding period before harvest for feeding.

## Leaf rust
Cause: fungus (Cerotelium fici). Symptoms: orange to reddish-brown pustules on the lower leaf surface, severe cases cause leaf drying and fall. Mitigation: prune and burn infected leaves, avoid late-evening irrigation, apply recommended fungicide with the label waiting period.

## Leaf blight
Symptoms: brown irregular blotches from leaf margins, leaves curl and shrivel in humid weather. Mitigation: remove affected shoots, improve drainage, avoid dense planting, spray recommended fungicide.

## Mosaic and viral-like symptoms
Symptoms: mottled light and dark green patches, crinkled leaves, stunted growth; often spread by sucking insects such as whitefly. Mitigation: control insect vectors, uproot severely affected plants, use disease-free cuttings for new plantation.

## Healthy leaf
Healthy leaves are uniformly dark green, glossy, thick and free of spots, powder or pustules. Leaf quality drives silkworm health and cocoon yield.
