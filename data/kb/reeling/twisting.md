## Twisting
Raw silk yarn is doubled and twisted into structural thread (organzine and tram) according to the required denier and twist. Consistent yarn supply and uniform quality are essential for steady twisting output.

## Handling a demand surge
When demand or a target quantity rises, compare the target with current yarn stock, safety stock and the spare capacity of linked reelers. If the gap exceeds what current reelers can supply in time, search for alternative reelers, ideally near the mill to keep transport short, and agree a delivery plan.

## Safety stock
A spinner should hold yarn safety stock to cover supply variability during the lead time. When stock falls below the reorder point, place an order at once.
