## Environment
Young-age larvae need roughly 26-28 C with high relative humidity (80-90 percent); late-age larvae need about 23-26 C with 70-75 percent humidity and good airflow. Avoid sudden fluctuations and direct draught.

## Hygiene and disinfection
Disinfect the rearing house, trays and equipment before every batch and keep a footbath at the entrance. Clean beds regularly and remove litter and dead larvae immediately. Separate young-age and late-age rearing areas.

## Feeding
Feed good-quality fresh leaf at regular intervals: tender leaf for young larvae, mature leaf for late instars. Do not feed wet, dusty or pesticide-sprayed leaf. Poor leaf quality is a main trigger of flacherie.

## Harvest and mounting
At the ripe stage, larvae stop feeding and are mounted on clean mountages to spin. Cocoons are harvested about five to six days after spinning starts. Harvest promptly and take the cocoons to market or the reeler quickly.

## Planning with the reeler
The rearing schedule should be shared with the reeler so cocoon supply and reeling capacity match. If disease reduces the expected harvest, tell the reeler early so alternative cocoon sources can be arranged.
