## When should a farmer escalate to the reeler
Escalate when the expected cocoon loss is large (roughly more than a fifth of the batch), when a highly infectious disease (grasserie, pebrine) is confirmed, or when the harvest date will slip. Early notice lets the reeler secure alternative cocoons before their stock runs low.

## Human approval
Every deal or reroute proposed by Ariadne must be approved by a human before any balance or stock changes. If the evidence is thin, the system should say so and ask for expert help instead of guessing.
