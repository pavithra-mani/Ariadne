## First response when larvae look diseased but the disease is not yet identified
Every common silkworm disease (grasserie, flacherie, muscardine, pebrine) is infectious, so until an expert identifies it, treat the batch as infectious.
Silkworm disease isolate disinfect remove dead larvae hygiene steps:
- Remove sick and dead larvae immediately and burn them; do not throw them in the open or on the compost heap.
- Practise disinfection of the rearing house, surroundings, trays and appliances with a bleaching powder or formalin solution; wear a mask and gloves while disinfecting.
- Keep proper temperature and humidity, improve ventilation, avoid overcrowding, and feed only fresh, good-quality dry leaf.
- Clean the beds regularly and handle healthy trays before sick ones; disinfect between batches.
- Ask a sericulture extension officer or an expert to confirm the disease before treating or selling the batch; do not reuse eggs (layings) from an affected batch.
