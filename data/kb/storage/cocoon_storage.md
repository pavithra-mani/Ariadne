## Fresh cocoons are perishable
Live cocoons contain pupae. If they are not sold and stifled (heat-killed) within a few days, the moth emerges and pierces the cocoon, which makes it unusable for continuous reeling. Fresh cocoons should reach the reeler within a day and be stifled or reeled within about a week at most. Heat and moisture accelerate spoilage.

## Storage conditions
Spread cocoons in a thin layer on trays in a cool, well-ventilated, shaded room. Do not stack in sacks, do not expose to direct sun. Turn and inspect cocoons daily. Remove soiled and double cocoons separately.

## Transport
Transport cocoons in the cooler hours in ventilated baskets or trays, never tightly packed. Long delays on the road reduce cocoon quality and price, so keep transit time short.

## Stock planning
Reelers should keep a safety stock of cocoons that covers expected lead time of resupply and review the reorder point regularly. Cocoons close to the end of their shelf life must be processed first.
