## Licensing notes

- FAO manual: FAO's notice permits copies for personal or classroom use without fee, not for profit, with this notice and full citation; republication/posting on servers or redistribution needs FAO permission. Keep the files internal to the class project and cite FAO (Yong-woo Lee, FAO Agricultural Services Bulletin 136, 1999).
- Halagundegowda et al. (2021): CC BY 4.0, attribution required.
- Government of India (CSB SILKS portal, MoSPI): public government web content, no reuse restriction seen; attribute.
- INSERCO Sericologia articles, ALCP2 guideline, Cuban Journal review: openly downloadable without login, but no explicit reuse licence was found in the PDFs; used for non-commercial educational RAG with attribution. Remove if the team needs strict licence clarity.
